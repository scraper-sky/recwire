RecWire

Wire your movies

RecWire suggests a film to watch from a map of the films you love and hate.

Help

Where do my picks come from? Every film wired to Tonight's recommendation shapes your picks. Films closer to it count more, and films in a cluster count double. Tap a pick under the map to see which film led to it.

How do I remove a wire? On the Movie Map, tap the wire, then tap Remove.

How do I import from Letterboxd? On letterboxd.com, go to Settings, then Data, and export your data. Then in RecWire, go to Your films, tap Import from Letterboxd and choose the .zip file.

Where is my data? On your phone only. See the privacy policy.
