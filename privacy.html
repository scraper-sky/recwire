RecWire

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

The short version

RecWire has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your films, ratings and Movie Map are stored on your phone, and we don't run servers that receive them.

What stays on your phone

  • The films you rate, save, mark as seen or put on your Movie Map
  • How you wire your map, and your daily picks
  • Settings, such as reminder times

Deleting the app deletes all of this. We can't see it or restore it.

The Movie Database (TMDB)

RecWire gets film details, posters and streaming availability from TMDB. To do that, your phone sends TMDB the film searches you type, the films being looked up, and your phone's country or region so it can show where to watch. As with any web request, TMDB also sees your IP address. TMDB's use of this is covered by the TMDB privacy policy.

Tapping where to watch a film opens TMDB's website. Streaming availability is provided by JustWatch.

Letterboxd import

When you import a Letterboxd export, the file is read on your phone. Only film titles and release years are sent to TMDB to find the matching films. The file isn't uploaded anywhere.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

  • Tonight for two creates a link containing a code for the films you've rated and how much you like them (up to 60 films). It doesn't include your name or anything else about you. It only goes to whoever you send it to.
  • Sharing your map creates an image you can send with the iOS share sheet.

Notifications

Movie night reminders are scheduled on your phone. RecWire doesn't use push notifications, so no notification data is sent to us.

Children

RecWire isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
